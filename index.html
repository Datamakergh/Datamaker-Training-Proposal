Programme outcomes · Training of Trainers

What You Will Be Able To Do

Eight outcomes across three days. Day 1 covers the first three and introduces the fourth.

1

Explain the fundamentals of AI data labeling and its role in developing AI systems.

2

Demonstrate core annotation workflows using the SYNAPSE platform, subject to project configuration and access.

3

Interpret annotation guidelines and explain task requirements to trainees.

4

Apply standard quality-control, error-correction and calibration procedures.

5

Plan and monitor a data-labeling assignment, including staffing, allocation, productivity and deadlines.

6

Maintain project records, production trackers, issue logs and progress reports.

7

Identify risks, escalate unresolved issues and communicate operational changes appropriately.

8

Design and deliver a structured training session and assess trainee competency.

DAY 1 FOCUSOutcomes 1 to 3, with the first exposure to outcome 8 through the trainer’s role and the paired practical.

Programme outcomes · Labeler Training

What You Will Be Able To Do

Eight outcomes across five days of hands-on AI data-labelling training.

1

Explain what AI data labelling is and why accurate labels matter.

2

Navigate an annotation tool and use MS Office for daily work.

3

Read a project guideline and apply its labelling rules.

4

Label images accurately with basic-level annotations.

5

Complete high-level annotations (Semantic, PCD).

6

Check quality standards and correct errors based on feedback.

7

Use Generative AI tools and write clear, effective prompts.

8

Build and present a simple website or tool using vibe coding.

BY DAY 5Outcomes 1–6 prepare a job-ready labeler
Outcomes 7–8 add AI skills for wider digital work.
